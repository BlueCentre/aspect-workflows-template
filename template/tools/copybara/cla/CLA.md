## How to sign

You sign this CLA **once**, on your first pull request. When you open a PR, the CLA
Assistant bot comments with instructions. To sign, post this exact comment on your
pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded in `signatures/version1/cla.json` on the
`cla-signatures` branch. Subsequent pull requests are recognized automatically.
